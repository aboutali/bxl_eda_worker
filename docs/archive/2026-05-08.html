Today

US forces struck Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz overnight following Iranian attacks on three US destroyers and intercepted strikes on the UAE, though Trump maintains the ceasefire holds and is touting a 14-point plan he claims Tehran may accept under economic pressure; Von der Leyen coordinated with Trump by phone on the regional response. The Commission is preparing additional EU sanctions listings against extremist Israeli settlers alongside a €6m West Bank monitoring program — a file Bern will need to assess for autonomous alignment — while signing fresh financing agreements with Jordan and launching the TAJDID water project in Jenin with France and Germany. EEAS previewed the 11–12 May Foreign Affairs and Defence Council meetings, where both dossiers will dominate.
