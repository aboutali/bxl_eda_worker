Today

The EU Foreign Affairs Council in Brussels cleared the way for sanctions on Israeli settlers tied to West Bank violence after Hungary's new PM Magyar lifted the Orbán-era veto, with HR/VP Kallas confirming approval alongside a fresh Russia package — a measure Bern will need to weigh for autonomous alignment. Kallas separately rejected Putin's proposal that former Chancellor Schröder mediate on Ukraine's behalf, while Commissioner Lahbib met Lebanese President Aoun on stability and reconstruction, and ministers held parallel talks with Syrian officials. On the broader Middle East track, Tehran's belated reply to the US ceasefire proposal was dismissed by Trump as "totally unacceptable," and Senator Kelly publicly flagged depleted US munitions stocks — context for ongoing European debate, including calls in Italy and Germany to suspend the EU-Israel Association Agreement. In parallel, US and Chinese officials met in South Korea to prepare a Trump-Xi summit on trade.
