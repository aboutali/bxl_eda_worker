Today

The Foreign Affairs Council delivered two consequential sanctions decisions: a new Russia listing of 16 individuals and 7 entities tied to the forced deportation of Ukrainian children, timed to the high-level meeting of the International Coalition for the Return of Ukrainian Children opened by HR/VP Kallas; and, after Budapest dropped the Orbán-era veto under new PM Magyar, listings against radical Israeli West Bank settlers, drawing sharp protest from Israel's foreign minister — both packages Bern will need to weigh for autonomous alignment. In parallel, the Council restored full application of the 1977 EU-Syria Cooperation Agreement and lifted trade restrictions, with the Commission co-hosting a Syria Partnership Coordination Forum in Brussels with the Transitional Authorities to channel reconstruction aid and enable refugee returns, though analysts caution conditions for safe repatriation are not yet met. Regional risk remained elevated as Trump threatened renewed military action against Iran amid Strait of Hormuz incidents, while NZZ flagged deepening Iran-North Korea cooperation potentially extending to nuclear assistance. Further afield, a Trump-Xi meeting in Beijing — the first such US presidential visit since 2017 — is being prepared via US-China talks in South Korea, against a Taiwan backdrop and the largest-ever Balikatan exercise featuring Japanese combat troops for the first time.
