Today

Brussels' agenda was framed by the EU Foreign Affairs Council in defence configuration on 12 May, where ministers reviewed defence priorities and HR/VP Kallas held a closing press conference; the debate over a common EU army amid US disengagement, and Slovakia's reassessment of tank procurement in light of drone-dominated battlefields in Ukraine, point to a procurement and doctrine shift Bern will want to track. On Ukraine, Kallas issued a statement at the International Coalition for the Return of Ukrainian Children and co-chaired a meeting with Canadian and Ukrainian counterparts, while the EU delivered an OSCE Permanent Council condemnation of Russia's continuing aggression — co-signature options Switzerland routinely joins — against the backdrop of one of Moscow's longest sustained strikes on Ukraine. Trump's arrival in Beijing with a CEO delegation (Musk, Cook, Fink) for talks with Xi on Iran and trade overshadowed a parallel EU-China conference held by the EU Delegation in Beijing, with reporting suggesting the Iran conflict is accelerating Chinese clean-tech exports and Beijing's leverage.
