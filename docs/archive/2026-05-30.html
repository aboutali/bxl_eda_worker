Today

Brussels moved on two parallel Middle East sanctions tracks, adding four entities and three individuals to the West Bank settler-violence regime while broadening the Hamas/PIJ framework with ten new listings and tighter asset-freeze and travel-ban measures — both files Bern will need to assess for autonomous alignment. The dual listings landed as Trump emerged from a two-hour Situation Room session without the promised "final determination" on Iran and threatened renewed strikes, with NZZ flagging that markets may be underpricing Hormuz-linked winter gas risks for Switzerland and Germany. At the Gymnich, HR/VP Kallas fronted the foreign ministers' discussions while the Council greenlit a seventh Ukraine Facility tranche of nearly €2.8 billion and delivered a UNSC statement on Ukraine; Costa concurrently travelled to the Western Balkans summit and the EU–Mexico leaders' meeting expanded trade and security cooperation. On China, Berlin signalled openness to tougher EU trade defence instruments — potentially unlocking harder Brussels action — as the College held an orientation debate on the relationship and Hegseth pressed Asian allies in Singapore to raise defence spending.
