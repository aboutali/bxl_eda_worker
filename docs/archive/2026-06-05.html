Today

The US House passed a Ukraine military aid and Russia sanctions package over Trump's objections, with 18 Republican defectors — a measure Bern will need to track for autonomous alignment under the Embargo Act, particularly as Putin used the St. Petersburg forum to restate maximalist demands on Donbas and the IAEA brokered a narrow ceasefire for repairs at Zaporizhzhia. In parallel, Macron and Merz travelled to Montenegro to push accelerated enlargement while Brussels weighs a "light membership" track for Kyiv, sharpening the question of Swiss positioning on EU geopolitical integration instruments. A separate US sanctions tranche targeting President Díaz-Canel and forcing European firms out of Cuba by Friday raises immediate extraterritorial exposure for Swiss-based entities and complicates any FDFA response on secondary-sanctions risk. The EEAS condemned Iranian strikes on civilian infrastructure in Kuwait and Bahrain — a statement Bern could plausibly co-sign — while the Council adopted a €100 million EPF measure for the Lebanese Armed Forces, timed to the Washington-brokered Israel-Lebanon ceasefire whose implementation in the south remains uncertain. A UN report adding Russia to the blacklist of conflict-related sexual violence perpetrators, citing systematic abuse of Ukrainian male detainees, reinforces the documentary basis for further restrictive measures.
