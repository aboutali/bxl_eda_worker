Today

Brussels held the EU-Western Balkans Summit in Tivat, with Costa and von der Leyen framing enlargement as geopolitical priority and Podgorica positioned as the lead accession track; in parallel, officials floated a "light membership" formula for Ukraine to shortcut the standard decade-long path, a debate Bern will want to track given its own EU dossier. On Russia, Putin used the St. Petersburg forum to reject talks with Zelenskyy and reiterate maximalist demands for full Donbas control, even as Ukrainian long-range strikes degrade Russian fuel logistics and trigger Crimea shortages; the EU reinforced its line at the OSCE Permanent Council, a statement Switzerland typically aligns with, and the EBRD-EU rolled out new private-sector and SME financing for Ukraine. Trade and tech tensions with China sharpened as Šefčovič heads to Beijing on the deficit file while China denounced the EU's new tech sovereignty plan on cloud, AI and chips as protectionist, with Xi's upcoming Pyongyang visit adding a Korean-peninsula dimension ahead of the 10 June EU-South Korea summit. On the southern neighbourhood, the Council adopted a €100 million European Peace Facility measure for the Lebanese Armed Forces and the EIB released a second €500 million tranche for Morocco's post-quake reconstruction.
