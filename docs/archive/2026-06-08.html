Today

The Iran–Israel ceasefire brokered by Washington in April collapsed as Tehran launched missiles at northern Israel and Israeli airstrikes hit Iranian cities, with both sides ignoring Trump's calls for restraint and the US president floating possible ground troop deployment; Israel separately resumed strikes on Beirut, and Bern will need to track whether the FAC convenes any aligned position. On NATO's eastern flank, allied jets downed a Russian drone in Latvian airspace while another struck a spent-fuel facility near Chernobyl, reinforcing the incursion pattern Switzerland has previously echoed in statements. On the Ukraine track, Putin is using Schröder and a Russian billionaire as informal channels to probe peace terms as Zelensky coordinates with Berlin, Paris and London, while Germany and France floated an "associate membership" tier for Kyiv and the Western Balkans and the Commission disbursed nearly €2.8 billion in macro-financial support. In Brussels, HR/VP Kallas chairs an informal defence ministers' meeting in Cyprus, the Council adopted a second EPF measure for the Egyptian Armed Forces, and NZZ reports Pentagon concern over intensified Israeli intelligence collection on US officials amid Netanyahu's difficulty reading Trump's Iran posture.
