Today

US forces struck Iranian military sites near the Strait of Hormuz after a US combat helicopter was downed, triggering Iranian missile retaliation against Bahrain, Kuwait and Jordan, even as Trump insisted an Iran deal remains "still close" — a regional escalation that framed an extraordinary IAEA Board of Governors meeting where the EU delivered a coordinated statement on Iran alongside separate interventions on North Korea safeguards and the 2025 Safeguards Implementation Report, positioning Bern will want to track for any co-signature opportunity. The Commission tabled a new Russia sanctions package proposing visa bans on FIDE chief Dvorkovich and Patriarch Kirill, which Switzerland will need to assess for autonomous alignment, while data showing nearly 97% of Yamal LNG cargoes still landing at EU ports exposes enforcement gaps in the March short-term contract ban. Draft European Council conclusions for next week notably omit China despite rising trade frictions, even as Seoul's president arrived in Brussels for the first EU–South Korea summit in three years covering chips, defence and Beijing. The EU also co-signed a joint statement urging a political track in Sudan, concluded an expanded EPA with the ESA4 states, and — via HR/VP Kallas in Dublin and at an informal defence ministers' meeting — kept Ukraine accession and defence-industrial files moving, with first negotiation chapters reportedly slated to open 15 June despite Hungarian and Polish reservations.
