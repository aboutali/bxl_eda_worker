Today

A second consecutive day of US airstrikes on Iran dominated the agenda, with Tehran threatening to close the Strait of Hormuz and retaliate against US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, lifting oil prices and tightening copper and aluminium markets already under strain; Trump signaled further escalation as nuclear talks stalled and Iran refused to engage. In parallel, Commission President von der Leyen unveiled the EU's 21st sanctions package against Russia, targeting the shadow fleet, banks, crypto and entry bans on Russians who served in Ukraine, alongside visa bans on FIDE chief Dvorkovich and Patriarch Kirill — a package Bern will need to assess for autonomous alignment. Brussels suffered two diplomatic setbacks as China abruptly cancelled two sets of high-level talks amid trade frictions, while member states floated breaking up the EEAS over dissatisfaction with Kaja Kallas's coordination. On Ukraine, Zelensky will join the G7 session in Evian on Tuesday, sanctioned oligarch Abramovich resurfaced as a Kyiv-Moscow back-channel, and a car bomb near Moscow killed a senior Russian general overseeing missiles and artillery. The EU also co-signed a joint statement urging a civilian-led political transition in Sudan — a text Switzerland could plausibly join — and concluded a digital trade agreement with South Korea at their summit.
