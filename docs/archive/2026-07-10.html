Today

The renewed US-Iran war, ongoing since February, dominated: Trump declared the ceasefire ended at the NATO summit as US forces struck over 170 targets following attacks on merchant ships, placing control of the Strait of Hormuz at the center of the conflict, while Ayatollah Khamenei was buried in Mashhad amid succession questions pointing to his son Mojtaba and warnings of spillover to Gulf states hosting US bases in Bahrain and Kuwait. In Brussels, the European Council adopted conclusions on Ukraine and European defence and security, aligned with NATO's designation of Russia as a long-term threat and preparation of a roughly $70bn Ukraine support package, developments Bern should track for defence-cooperation and posture implications. The Commission circulated an options paper on restricting trade with Israeli West Bank settlements ahead of Monday's Foreign Affairs Council, a measure Switzerland will need to assess for alignment should ministers proceed. The Commission and HR-VP also proposed a horizontal sanctions regime targeting migrant smuggling, human trafficking, and organised-crime networks including firearms, drugs, and money laundering—a further autonomous-measure file relevant to Swiss alignment decisions. Kallas is due at the NATO summit in Ankara, and the EU issued a joint statement on amendments to the 2018 South Sudan peace agreement open to co-signature.
