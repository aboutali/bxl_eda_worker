Today

US-Iran hostilities dominated, with US forces striking Iranian targets for a third consecutive night, reinstating a naval blockade of Iranian ports, and notifying Congress of a state of war that starts a 60-day War Powers clock; Trump's demand for a 20% fee on Strait of Hormuz cargo and Tehran's closure of the strait and retaliation against Gulf states and Jordan raise direct exposure for maritime shipping, prompting a joint EU-Bahrain (GCC presidency) statement on freedom of navigation that Bern may consider aligning with. On sanctions, EU foreign ministers failed to approve the 21st Russia package ahead of a Wednesday deadline to finalize an oil price cap, a file Switzerland will track for autonomous adoption, while the Council did expand the Sudan regime to target conflict-financing gold trade but declined to designate the UAE-backed RSF. A majority of ministers backed banning trade with illegal Israeli settlements, but unanimity requirements and Commission-level divisions leave adoption uncertain, a measure Switzerland would weigh separately if enacted. The Council added four individuals and five entities over digital surveillance in Russia, and the HR-VP issued statements with Ukraine on civilian detainees and against Russia's cyber activity, alongside an EU-UK confirmation of British participation in the Ukraine Support Loan. Two senior Swiss diplomats are visiting Syria and Lebanon as Bern seeks a diplomatic and humanitarian role amid the regional escalation.
