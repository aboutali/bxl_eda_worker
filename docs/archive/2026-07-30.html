Today

The EU adopted its 21st Russia sanctions package, targeting energy, financial services and crypto, though the final text was weakened to permit continued Greek imports of Russian LNG; Bern faces a near-term alignment decision, with the HR/VP separately confirming third-country adherence to existing measures on Russia, cyber-attacks, human rights, Sudan and Lebanon. The package landed as Russian strikes killed eight in Ukraine and Zelensky met Trump in Washington amid analysis pointing to a Trump-Zelensky split over whether the Russia-Iran axis makes Moscow a US adversary. In parallel, the US struck Iranian soil in response to an Iranian missile attack on American forces in Jordan, with the conflict extending to US and Saudi strikes on pro-Iranian militias in Iraq before Trump announced a pause to enable negotiations; the EU High Representative issued a statement on Iran whose content was not specified. Two items warrant Swiss legal attention: leaked advice shows Commission negotiators pursued a Europol-Israel data-sharing deal despite warnings it breached EU and international law and bypassed member states, while Israeli firms are advertising West Bank settlement exports to Spain in apparent defiance of Madrid's ban. Separately, the EU disbursed €1.5 billion to Egypt under its Macro-Financial Assistance programme.
