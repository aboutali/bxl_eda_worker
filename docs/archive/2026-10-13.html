Today

The EU adopted its largest set of sanctions listings, targeting close to 1,600 individuals and entities linked to Russia's military-industrial base, alongside separate Council measures sanctioning 77 people over the illegal State Duma elections in occupied Ukrainian territories, adding three individuals and four entities to the DPRK regime over nuclear, missile and Russia-related ties, and renewing the chemical-weapons framework for another year — all package elements Bern will need to assess for autonomous alignment.
