Today

Commission President von der Leyen announced a 22nd sanctions package against Russia, including a proposed ban on Russian teams, flag-flying athletes and official Russian symbols at European sports events, which dovetails with a call from seven member states for an EU visa ban on Russian and Belarusian athletes; Bern will need to assess alignment once the Council adopts the package.
