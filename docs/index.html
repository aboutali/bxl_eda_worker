Brussels also hosted the 11th EU-Korea Summit, producing a digital trade agreement covering data flows alongside a joint statement on bilateral and geopolitical cooperation, and the EU co-signed a multilateral statement urging a civilian-led political track in Sudan — both texts Switzerland may consider endorsing.
