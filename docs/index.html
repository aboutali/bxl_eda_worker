US forces conducted a second consecutive day of airstrikes on Iran, prompting Tehran to threaten retaliation against US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain and to warn of closing the Strait of Hormuz to all shipping, with oil prices climbing on conflicting signals over the waterway's status and reports of 20,000 left without water after strikes on reservoir infrastructure.
